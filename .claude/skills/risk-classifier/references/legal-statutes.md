# Legal Reference (for deep analysis)

- **LDA**: lobbying registration required if >$3,000 in activities/quarter
- **Revolving door**: 1-year (2-year senior officials) cooling-off period
- **FARA**: Foreign Agents Registration Act — complements `foreign_entities` fields
- **18 USC 201**: bribery of public officials
- **18 USC 1346**: honest services fraud

Flag potential violations in findings with `legal_violation_flag: true` for evaluation panel review.
